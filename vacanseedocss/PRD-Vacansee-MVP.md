Product Requirements Document: VacanSee MVPOverviewProduct Name: VacanSeeProblem Statement: Finding available boarding house accommodations is currently inefficient due to a heavy reliance on manual "walk-around" searching and outdated social media information. This creates "information hunger" for students and leads to redundant, time-consuming inquiries for property owners.MVP Goal: To establish a centralized web-based platform where boarding house owners can update room availability in real-time and students can filter verified listings by budget and location.Target Launch: Academic Year 2025-2026 (Development cycle spanning 2024-2025).Target UsersPrimary User ProfileWho: University students (primarily at USTP) and local boarding house owners in Cagayan de Oro City.Problem: Students waste hours visiting "full" houses; owners are interrupted by constant inquiries for rooms that are no longer available.Current Solution: Physical signboards, personal referrals, and unmaintained Facebook group posts.Why They'll Switch: Real-time accuracy. Users gain immediate certainty about vacancy status without needing to travel or make phone calls.User Persona: Alex (Student Tenant)Demographics: 18–22 years old, student in Cagayan de Oro, budget-conscious.Tech Level: Advanced (Mobile-first, frequent user of web apps).Goals: Find a secure, affordable room within walking distance of campus before the semester starts.Frustrations: Seeing a "For Rent" post online only to find out the room was taken three days ago.User JourneyThe StoryAlex needs a new boarding house for the upcoming semester. Instead of walking the streets in the heat, Alex opens VacanSee. Alex filters by "Male Dorm" and a budget of "P3,000 - P5,000." The system shows three verified vacancies near the university. Alex views photos of the rooms, checks the amenities, and clicks "Request Booking" for the preferred option. The owner receives a notification, confirms the vacancy is still open, and Alex receives a prompt to visit for a final viewing, knowing the spot is reserved.Key TouchpointsDiscovery: University bulletin boards, student group chats, and social media announcements.First Contact: Landing page showing a search bar and featured nearby listings.Onboarding: Quick account creation (Tenant or Owner) with email verification.Core Loop: Owners toggle "Vacant/Occupied" status; Tenants search and filter listings.Retention: Reliable notifications for students looking for housing and a streamlined management tool for owners.MVP FeaturesCore Features (Must Have)1. Real-Time Vacancy ToggleDescription: A simple dashboard switch for owners to change a room's status from "Vacant" to "Occupied."User Value: Ensures tenants only see rooms that are actually available.Success Criteria:Owners can update status in under two clicks.Changes reflect instantly on the public search results.Data state is synced across the central database.Priority: Critical2. Search & Multi-Criteria FilteringDescription: A search interface allowing tenants to filter by price range, gender orientation (Male/Female/Mixed), and specific amenities.User Value: Saves time by hiding irrelevant listings.Success Criteria:Results update dynamically based on filter selection.Listings display key details (price, location, vacancy count) prominently.Priority: High3. Property Management & Photo UploadsDescription: A profile section where owners can list property details and upload images of the rooms.User Value: Builds trust and allows tenants to "visit" the room virtually.Success Criteria:Owners can upload and delete images.Essential data fields (Rent price, amenities) are mandatory for listing.Priority: High4. Booking Request SystemDescription: A communication bridge where tenants can express interest and owners can accept or decline inquiries.User Value: Initiates the rental process without requiring immediate phone calls.Success Criteria:Notifications are sent to owners upon request.Tenants receive status updates (Pending/Accepted/Declined).Priority: MediumFuture Features (Not in MVP)FeatureWhy
